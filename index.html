About The Project

ABOUT KKR GREEN CITY

Nestled in a tranquil locale in Eluru, KKR Green City offers harmonious residential living enveloped by vast green vistas.

Surrounded by open paddy landscapes and refreshing breeze, KKR Green City has been carefully envisioned for families seeking clean air, calm morning strolls, and an unhurried lifestyle away from commercial congestion.

With wide internal roads, serene meditation zones, open grounds, and close proximity to public transport at Mini Bypass, this enclave balances peaceful retreat with effortless day-to-day accessibility.
